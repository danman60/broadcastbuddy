Glides the PTZ from one saved preset to another. The camera cannot slow its own preset recall, so a genuinely slow move needs WAYPOINTS: save extra presets along the path (in-app grid or Cam Pos keys), list their ids here in order, and the move paces through them hop-by-hop over the full duration. Without waypoints the move is snap-to-From, dwell, then one native-speed recall to To.
